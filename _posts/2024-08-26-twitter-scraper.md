While the **Twitter Scraper Tool** offers powerful capabilities, it’s crucial to use it responsibly. Ethical considerations should be at the forefront of your usage:

- **Respect Privacy**: Always respect the privacy of Twitter users. Do not use the tool to collect or disseminate sensitive information.

- **Avoid Spammy Practices**: While the tool allows for mass outreach, it’s essential to avoid spammy or misleading practices. Quality engagement is more valuable than quantity; focus on building genuine connections with your audience.
