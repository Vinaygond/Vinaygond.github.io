I like the part of software engineering where a real-world process becomes something people can understand and use: a model with explicit states, a useful screen, a calculation that can be reproduced, and a failure that does not disappear behind a success message.

## What I am working on

I founded **Exit Protocol**, an evidence-linked financial investigation platform. My current focus is the preparation desk: organizing financial productions, showing accounts and represented periods, and keeping review decisions connected to the source.

More analysis is not helpful if the reviewer still cannot find the right statement.

## What changed how I build

My earlier automation product encountered platform enforcement. I have written a [postmortem](/2025-12-01-The-500K-Tool-That-Got-Me-a-Cease-and-Desist-from-X/) rather than treating the incident as a badge of honor.

Throughput cannot compensate for weak authorization boundaries or shared customer risk. I now pay closer attention to what an integration is permitted to do, how decisions are recorded, and what a public claim actually demonstrates.
